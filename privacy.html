Privacy Policy

CoachZ stores all of your data on your device, and nowhere else.

  • No accounts. CoachZ has no sign-up, login, or user profiles on any server.
  • No data collection. Your workouts, food logs, body weight, measurements, sleep, water, supplements, and progress photos are saved only in your device's local browser storage. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or synced anywhere.
  • No analytics or tracking. CoachZ contains no analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, cookies for tracking, or third-party trackers of any kind.
  • Backups are yours. The optional "Export backup" feature creates a file on your device that you control entirely. CoachZ never sees it.
  • Optional sharing. The "Share this week" feature generates an image on your device and hands it to your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.
  • Deleting your data. Use "Erase all data" in Settings, or uninstall the app / clear the site's browser storage. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete on any server.

If this policy ever changes, the change will be published on this page before it takes effect.
